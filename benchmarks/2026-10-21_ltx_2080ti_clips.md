# LTX on an RTX 2080 Ti: bf16 vs fp16 VAE (2026-10-21)

Rented RTX 2080 Ti 22 GB (modded, host caps it at 150 W, so a stock card is somewhat faster), Linux,
plaintext models, CLI from source, Standard model (2 steps), 512 canvas, all 48 blocks resident. Old =
main `6ebb294`, new = `64d823f` (fp16 VAE before SM 8.0). Old ran with a warm compile cache; new ran
once cold (first job after install) and once warm.

| | m506-1 (20 s, 7 windows) old | new (warm) | m284-1 (15 s, 5 windows) old | new (warm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole job | 364.1 s | 180.5 s (2.0x faster) | 257.5 s | 126.6 s (2.0x faster) |
| Scan | 14.9 s | 14.8 s | 8.4 s | 8.3 s |
| Encode | 145.1 s (19.8 s/window) | 25.7 s (2.0 s/window) | 101.4 s (20.1 s/window) | 16.4 s (2.0 s/window) |
| Denoise | 57.3 s (3.80 s/step) | 55.5 s (3.77 s/step) | 42.2 s (3.78 s/step) | 39.6 s (3.67 s/step) |
| Compose | 141.5 s (decode 20.0 s/window) | 78.5 s (decode 10.8 s/window) | 100.3 s (decode 19.8 s/window) | 56.0 s (decode 10.7 s/window) |
| Peak VRAM (nvidia-smi) | 20.8 GB | 14.7 GB | 20.8 GB | 15.6 GB |

Cold first job: 306.2 s and 127.3 s. The extra time on the first video is the one-off native block
build and kernel compiles in an empty cache, about 90 s of it in the denoise.

Cause: before SM 8.0 a bf16 Conv3d has no cuDNN kernel and falls back to an im2col GEMM, and bf16 GEMMs
have no tensor cores, so the bf16 VAE spent ~80 % of the LTX time. The denoise was already INT8 and is
unchanged. Per window at 512: encode 19.8 -> 2.0 s, decode 20.0 -> 10.8 s, denoise 7.6 s.
