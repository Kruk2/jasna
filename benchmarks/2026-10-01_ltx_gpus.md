# LTX speed per GPU (2026-10-01)

Trial weights (same speed as the real model). Denoise: `scripts/benchmark_ltx_denoise.py`, decode:
`scripts/benchmark_ltx_decode.py`. Commit per row in `2026-10-01_ltx_gpus.csv`.

## 512 px, distilled INT8

| GPU | denoise ms/step | decode s/window (segment / whole video) | s/window | blocks on GPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RTX 5090 32 GB | 491 | 1.12 / 0.86 | 4.8 | 48/48 |
| RTX 4090 24 GB | 655 | 1.71 / 1.33 | 6.6 | 48/48 |
| RTX 5080 16 GB | 962 | 2.25 / 1.75 | 9.5 | 44/48 |
| RTX 3090 24 GB | 1541 | 3.30 / 2.55 | 15 | 48/48 |
| RTX 2080 Ti 11 GB | 3137 | 22.6 / 17.7 | 43–48 | 25/48 |

s/window = 8 denoise steps + whole-video decode.

## Other modes, denoise ms/step

Decode at 768, segment / whole video: 5090 2.83 / 1.92 s, 5080 5.71 / 4.57 s, 4090 4.25 / 2.94 s,
3090 8.34 / 5.61 s, 2080 Ti 59.5 s.

30 s 1080p clip end to end on the 5090: INT8 147 s, NVFP4 96 s, undistilled 205 s.

Power: 5080 capped at 300 W, 3090 at 350 W, 2080 Ti locked at 1350 MHz; stock cards are a bit faster.
RTX 20 has no fast bf16 path, so decode there is about 20× a 5090.
