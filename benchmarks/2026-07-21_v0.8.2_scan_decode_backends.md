# Every-frame mosaic scan benchmark — decode backends

- Date: 2026-07-21
- Version: 0.8.2 (unreleased)
- **BASELINE: the `vali` column is the reference baseline for future scan runs.**

## Setup

- Script: `scripts/benchmark_scan_backends.py` — GUI `MosaicScanWorker` with `stride_seconds=0.0` (detection on every frame, results into one preallocated GPU tensor, one CPU sync at the end). Detector build + engine compile excluded from timing; wall covers decode + detection + final sync.
- Settings: GUI defaults (rfdetr-v6, fp16, batch 4)
- Clips: same set as the e2e benchmark (`assets/benchmark/`, see e2e doc)
- RAM/VRAM: med/peak of the benchmark subprocess (0.5s sampling, `scripts/bench_memory.py`)

## Conclusions

1. Unlike full e2e, the scan is decode-sensitive: vali beats pyav-hw by 5-8% at ≤1080p, 13-31% at 4K (biggest: av1 4K +31%), 22% at 8K VR.
2. pyav-sw is 2-5x slower — software decode fully bottlenecks a detection-only pass.
3. vali also uses ~100-350 MB less VRAM than pyav-hw on every clip.
4. Scan throughput (vali): ~830-860 fps at ≤1080p, ~490-720 fps at 4K, ~170 fps at 8K VR.
