# E2E: CUDA graphs + fp8 upsample sub-engine (2026-07-27)

Source tree v0.9.1 (source), RTX 5090, driver 595.84. Canonical setup: vali,
`--secondary-restoration none --temporal-overlap 15 --max-clip-size 180`,
6 SONE-610 bench clips, medians of 3 runs (`scripts/benchmark_decode_backends.py`).

Configs (experimental env hooks in `jasna/restorer/basicvsrpp_sub_engines.py`):

- **graphs** — `JASNA_TRT_CUDAGRAPHS=1`: CUDA graphs for the 4 static loop_body
  sub-engines, captured once at session build before pipeline threads start
  (capture poisons concurrent TRT calls from other threads — the graphs mode is
  toggled on only around the propagate loop; replays are thread-safe).
  Bit-exact output.
- **graphs+fp8** — additionally `JASNA_UPSAMPLE_ENGINE_OVERRIDE=` fp8 b180
  upsample engine (dev-side modelopt calibration; 63.9 dB / SSIM 0.9998 vs
  fp32 on synthetic clips).

- CUDA graphs: **−2…−10% wall e2e**, larger at lower resolutions (restoration
  cost is resolution-independent 256² crops, so its share of the critical path
  shrinks as decode/encode grow at 4K). VRAM unchanged.
- fp8 upsample adds **0…−2.5% wall** on top and cuts **~1 GB median / up to
  1.8 GB peak VRAM** (smaller activation footprint of the fp8 b180 engine).
- Peak fps: 1080p 167→181, 720p 171→192, 4K 90→96.
